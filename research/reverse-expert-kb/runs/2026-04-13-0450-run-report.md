A second compact split reinforced this run:

```text

  != operation escaped cancellation
  != owner was still live at delivery time
  != downstream consumer accepted it as current work

```

Concrete practical reinforcements preserved in the KB:
- Boost.Asio timer-cancel docs explicitly preserve the race where handlers may already have been invoked or queued, so cancel request is weaker than handler fate
- gRPC `TryCancel()` is best-effort, does not guarantee cancellation, and does not remove pending completion-queue tags; tag delivery and `ok` classification are separate proof objects
- Python `asyncio.wait_for()` documents that timeout waits until the future is actually cancelled, so timeout observation is weaker than full retirement completion
- Tokio `timeout(...)` documents that a non-yielding future may exceed the timeout without error, so deadline machinery is weaker than cooperative task fate
