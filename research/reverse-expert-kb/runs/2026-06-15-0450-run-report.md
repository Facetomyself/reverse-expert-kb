## New findings
- `FwpsCalloutRegister*` proves callout registration and callback entry-point existence, not that a management filter references the callout or that the target traffic can reach it.
- `FwpmCalloutAdd*` / `FwpmFilterAdd*` and layer/condition/weight state are separate from `classifyFn` delivery; a filter can exist at a layer whose semantics do not match the analyst's claim.
- `classifyFn` entry proves only a delivered classify indication with layer-specific fixed values, metadata, raw `layerData`, filter information, and optional flow context. It is weaker than a captured `classifyOut->actionType` write.
- `FWPS_CLASSIFY_OUT*` decision truth depends on `actionType`, `rights`, and `flags`; `FWP_ACTION_CONTINUE`, missing `FWPS_RIGHT_ACTION_WRITE`, or `FWPS_CLASSIFY_OUT_FLAG_ABSORB` can make a naive block/permit claim wrong.
- Asynchronous classify changes the proof object: ALE uses pend/complete plus possible reauthorization; packet and stream paths can block+absorb, clone/reference data, then reinject or discard later. Pended/cloned/absorbed traffic is not final behavior proof until completion/reinjection/discard and effect are correlated.
- ALE layer semantics matter: connect, receive/accept, flow-established, endpoint closure, redirect, and discard layers answer different questions. Flow-established is tracking-oriented; discard layers are often logging after a drop; redirect layers are address/port mutation seams.
- The compact split added this run is: **registered != filter-matched != classify-entered != decision-written != async-owned != completed/reinjected/discarded != effect-owned**.

## Reflections / synthesis
The native branch now has several policy/evidence seams: ETW for telemetry, EndpointSecurity for macOS security events, and Windows minifilters for file-system policy drivers. WFP is the network-filter counterpart and deserves its own workflow because its false stops are layer-shaped and async-shaped rather than file-operation-shaped.

The practical correction is:

```text
WFP callout registered
  != BFE management filter/layer references the callout
  != this traffic matched conditions and delivered classifyFn
  != classifyOut action/rights/flags encode a real decision
  != async pend/clone/absorb retained ownership correctly
  != completion, reauthorization, reinjection, or discard applied the decision
  != socket-visible, packet-visible, or policy-owned effect
```

This note should stop operators from overreading a WFP driver, callout table, `classifyFn` hit, or `BLOCK|ABSORB` write as final allow/block proof. It also creates clean handoffs: user-mode verdict services route to native IPC/service-worker notes; telemetry-only flows route to ETW/runtime-evidence; packet/stream mutation routes through clone/reinject/discard proof; malware/EDR/network-inspection cases freeze WFP ownership before reporting capability, detection, or consequence.
